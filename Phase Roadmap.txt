## Phase Roadmap (High Level)

| Phase | Focus | Deliverable |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **Phase 1** (this plan) | Foundation + Core POS | Login → Menu → Orders → Kitchen → Payment → Receipt |
| Phase 2 | Floor & Table Management | Floor editor, table status, dine-in workflow |
| Phase 3 | Inventory & Suppliers | Stock, recipes, ingredients, purchase orders |
| Phase 4 | Reporting & Analytics | Daily/monthly sales, tax reports, dashboards |
| Phase 5 | Customer & Loyalty | Customer database, loyalty points, gift cards |
| Phase 6 | Advanced Features | Shifts, cash drawer, daily closing, reservations |
| Phase 7 | Fiscal Compliance | Verifactu, TicketBAI, QR codes, AEAT integration |
| Phase 8 | Cloud & Multi-Branch | Multi-branch, franchise, advanced sync |
| Phase 9 | Integrations | Delivery platforms, accounting, payment gateways |
